Film + Art Nearby

All of these are easy to reach from Arlo Midtown and the theater district. Prices below are current adult/general-admission prices as of November 2026.

MoMA

$30

Best fit for your art interest, and especially good if you also care about film: MoMA has major modern-art galleries plus an active film program and screening theaters. 11 W 53rd St.

Official visit info ↗ Google Maps ↗

Paley Museum

$20

A very close media-history stop with television, film, archival screenings and rotating exhibitions. 25 W 52nd St — only a few blocks from the August Wilson.

Official visit info ↗ Google Maps ↗

Museum of Arts and Design

$20

Contemporary craft, design, objects and material-focused exhibitions at Columbus Circle. Good counterpoint to MoMA if you like production design and visual culture. 2 Columbus Circle.

Official visit info ↗ Google Maps ↗

Film at Lincoln Center

$18–19

Probably the best pure-film add-on: repertory cinema, restorations, new releases, festivals and filmmaker events. Standard series tickets are $18; new releases/festivals are $19, before online service fees.

Tickets & visit info ↗ Google Maps ↗

Museum of Broadway

$34–43

Not film, but a strong fit for this particular trip: immersive Broadway history, costumes, sets and backstage craft. Timed tickets generally run $34–43 before fees/tax. 145 W 45th St.
